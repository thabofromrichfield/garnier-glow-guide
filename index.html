A LITTLE REAL TALK

Skincare should
feel like care.

There isn't one right way to have skin. This guide is here to make exploring Garnier face care feel easier—not to label, diagnose or promise a transformation.

01

Keep it simple. Start with a cleanser, moisturizer and daytime sun protection that suit you.

02

Go at your pace. Introduce one new product at a time. Check the label and patch-test if you have concerns.

03

Ask a pro when needed. For persistent irritation or a skin concern, talk with a qualified healthcare professional.
